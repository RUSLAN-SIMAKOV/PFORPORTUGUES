FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
PT4UA is a simple, friendly way to learn European Portuguese – the Portuguese spoken in Portugal. It is made for Ukrainian speakers: all tasks and explanations are in Ukrainian.

HOW IT WORKS
• You see a sentence in Ukrainian and build the translation from word cards.
• You instantly see whether it is right and hear how the sentence sounds in Portuguese.
• Sentences you got wrong come back at the end of the lesson until you know them.
• Your progress is saved, so you can continue where you stopped.

21 LESSONS · 2,100 SENTENCES

Basics
• Present and past tense
• SER, ESTAR and TER
• Questions
• -ER and -IR verbs

Grammar
• The base scheme: past, present and future in statements, questions and negations
• Irregular verbs: SER, ESTAR, TER, IR, FAZER, DIZER, VIR, VER and more
• Modal verbs, pronouns, reflexive verbs
• Numbers, time and dates
• Continuous actions (estou a trabalhar) and the imperfect
• Imperative and subjunctive
• Conditional and comparisons

Life in Portugal
• At the doctor and the pharmacy
• Documents, bank and work: NIF, AIMA, Segurança Social
• Café, restaurant and shop
• Transport and the city
• Home life: renting, bills, repairs

RULES AT HAND
Every lesson has a short help screen with conjugation tables and tips.

PORTUGUESE AS SPOKEN IN PORTUGAL
All sentences use European Portuguese: autocarro, comboio, telemóvel, pequeno-almoço, "estou a fazer".

NO ADS, NO SIGN-UP
No account needed, no ads, works offline and collects no personal data.

Listening to sentences needs a Portuguese voice in your phone's text-to-speech settings (usually already installed).
